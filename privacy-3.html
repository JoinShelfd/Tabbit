Tabbit Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

Tabbit ("the app") is built around a simple principle: your data is yours and it stays on your device. This policy explains what the app does and doesn't do with information.

Data We Collect

None. Tabbit does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-ins, and no servers operated by us.

Data Stored on Your Device

Everything you enter in Tabbit is stored locally on your iPhone only, including:

  • Your name (if provided), drink habits, and prices you enter
  • Your log of skipped and purchased drinks
  • Savings goals, including any photo you attach to a goal
  • App preferences such as sounds, haptics, and reminders

This data never leaves your device through the app. Deleting the app, or using "Delete All Data" in Settings, permanently removes it. If you use iCloud device backups, your device may include Tabbit's local data in your personal backup — that backup belongs to you and is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Photos

If you attach a photo to a savings goal, Tabbit stores a copy of that photo locally on your device only. The app accesses only the photos you explicitly select.

Notifications

If you enable daily reminders, notifications are scheduled locally on your device. No notification data is sent to us.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment information. Purchase management and refunds are handled by Apple under Apple's terms.

Analytics and Tracking

Tabbit contains no third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or trackers.

Children's Privacy

Tabbit does not collect data from anyone, including children. The app is intended for users aged 13 and up.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new "Last updated" date.
